Common Questions

Q1: How do I place an order on Makibel Solution?

You can browse our partners' menu on the Services page, choose your desired items, enter your delivery address, and proceed to checkout.

Q2: How long does food delivery usually take?

Our average delivery time ranges between 30 to 45 minutes, depending on your distance from the restaurant and traffic conditions.

Q3: What payment methods do you support?

We accept cash on delivery, local mobile banking transfers, and major digital wallet payments.

Q4: How can my restaurant become a partner?

Go to our Contact Us page, fill out the partnership form or reach out directly to our support team to get started.
